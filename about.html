Portal Information

Educational Purpose

Solaris Ed was developed as a Senior High School Performance Task project. It serves as an independent interactive teaching and learning supplementary material for students and educators in TVL-ICT Animation.

Objectives

The Portal focuses on mastering the core animation concepts, encouraging it to apply through practical exercises and quizzes to test their knowledge. Additionally, students will learn to properly utilize industry-standard software tools and successfully produce, prepare, and export simple 2D or 3D animated clips.

Target Audience

Designed specifically for Senior High School learners and teachers, offering clear, structured, and student-friendly summaries of fundamental animation principles, digital asset management, and visual storytelling.
